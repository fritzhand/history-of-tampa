Demography & Time

City Growth vs. Downtown Hollow & Return

City of Tampa Population — Decennial Census (1850–2020)

Municipal population jumps track railroads, cigars, the 1920s land boom, wartime industry, and the 1953 annexation act: 140,331 of Tampa's 274,970 residents in 1960 lived in territory annexed after 1950. Citywide growth can mask downtown residential collapse.

Sources: 1960 Census Vol. I Pt. 11, Table 5 (1870–1960) · Gibson, POP-WP027 (1930–1990) · data.census.gov, 2020 P1 · 1850: Seventh Census, Statistics of Florida

Downtown-Core Residential Population

Core residents peak in the streetcar era, trough under urban renewal, then rebound with Harbour Island, Channel District lofts, and Water Street. 2010, 2020 and 2025 are Tampa Downtown Partnership district counts; earlier points are labeled estimates pending census-tract compilation.
